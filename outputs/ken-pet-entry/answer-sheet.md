## The proposed solution

Give the next vet a usable history without making the family reconstruct it. Each event distinguishes what was prescribed, bought, reportedly administered, or confirmed by a provider. Missing information stays visible. The first product is a new-vet handover; boarding and sitter handovers follow.

## 03 The six steps

**Required format:** One sentence per cell, no more than 15 words each. These are proposed product behaviors, not tested capabilities.

1. **Trigger:** An owner books a new vet and requests a handover.
2. **Already knows:** It knows the pet's shared records, caregiver contacts and permissions, keeping unverified details separate.
3. **Action:** It assembles the treatment timeline, separating prescriptions, purchases, reported care and provider confirmations.
4. **Counterparties:** It calls authorised previous providers to clarify gaps and sends the receiving vet a brief.
5. **Human decision:** It asks the owner before contacting providers or sharing records with a new recipient.
6. **Completion:** It finishes when the receiving vet acknowledges the brief, with unresolved facts explicitly flagged.
